Promo effect by store type

Counter-intuitive finding: Stores enrolled in the long-running "Promo2" campaign show 10.7% lower average sales than non-enrolled stores. Likely reverse causation — weaker-performing stores may be more likely to enroll in ongoing promotions, rather than promotions causing lower sales.
